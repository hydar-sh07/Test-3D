Neuroanatomical Atlas

Detailed anatomical breakdown of the primary Drosophila functional lobes.

👁️ Optic Lobes

Processes visual stimuli from compound eyes, motion detection, and spatial tracking. Comprises lamina, medulla, lobula, and lobula plate complexes.

🍄 Mushroom Bodies

The primary center for associative learning, olfactory memory formation, and sensory integration, harboring thousands of Kenyon cells.

🧭 Central Complex

Acts as the fly's internal compass and flight control computer, regulating spatial navigation, steering, and motor coordination.
